Lisbon by tram, day two

The 28 from Martim Moniz to Prazeres, sitting on the left.

The tram leaves Martim Moniz every twelve minutes, and every twelve minutes the queue is longer than the tram. Get on at the second stop instead, where locals board and the conductor waves the queue past.

Up through Graça the street narrows until the wing mirrors of parked cars fold in as the tram passes. Sit on the left side going west: the river shows between the buildings above Alfama, three times, each time for less than a breath.

Worth getting off for

  • Miradouro de Santa Luzia, before ten
  • The cathedral, for the cloister, not the nave
  • Estrela basilica and the garden opposite
  • Prazeres cemetery, at the end of the line

A day pass is €6.80 and pays for itself on the third ride. The ascensor da Glória is on it too, though the walk up is quicker if there is a queue.
